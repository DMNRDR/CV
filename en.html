I'm a full-stack developer with mud on my boots: half of my work happens on a PC in an editor, the other half on a construction site behind a total station or a drone controller. I never managed to pick a game — so I keep playing both my games.

Most of what I ship starts as a real problem with a deadline. A leather manufactory that needed an online catalogue with an ordering system. A delivery platform that grew from a landing page into couriers, dispatch and dashboards. A private map that refuses to put a server between friends — so the data lives nowhere but on the friends' own devices. Chatbots that handle transactions. And a lot more...

And when the computer screen turns off, I measure land and fly drones — building them, flying them, programming them. Same game, just a different altitude.
